Privacy Policy

Updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • The idea you submit, and the analysis Wazo produces for it.
  • Your name and email, only if you sign up to purchase Pro.
  • A hashed, salted version of your IP address (not the IP itself), used only to apply abuse-prevention rate limits.
  • Payment details, if you subscribe to Pro — handled entirely by Paddle.com, our payment processor. We never see or store your card details.

How it's used

Your idea text is sent to Anthropic's API to generate the analysis, and is not used by Anthropic to train models. Your idea and its analysis are stored on our servers (Supabase, hosted in the EU) so you can return to it. A copy is also kept in your browser's local storage on your own device.

If you sign up, your email is used to identify your Pro subscription and, only if you opt in, to send product updates. We never sell your data.

Who else sees it

  • Anthropic — processes your idea text to produce the analysis.
  • Supabase — hosts our database (EU region).
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — a privacy-preserving bot check; no tracking cookies.
  • Paddle.com — processes Pro subscription payments as merchant of record, and handles related billing/tax compliance.
  • Umami — cookie-free, anonymous analytics (page views only, no personal data).

Your rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time from inside the app (open the "Yours" drawer and use the delete-request option with the email you signed up with), or by emailing us directly. We'll confirm once it's done.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a data request: support@wazo.app.
