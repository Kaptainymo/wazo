1. What Wazo is

Wazo is a web app that produces an automated evaluation of a business idea you submit, using Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. The analysis is generated text, not professional business, legal, financial or investment advice. Numbers, company names and competitive claims can be wrong or out of date, since the model does not browse the web while it judges.

2. Using Wazo

You may use Wazo to analyze ideas you have the right to share. Don't submit content that is illegal, infringes someone else's rights, or attempts to misuse the service (for example, trying to use Wazo as a general-purpose chatbot unrelated to idea analysis, or automating abusive volumes of requests).

3. Accounts and plans

Running an idea through Wazo's free tier requires no account and has no usage limit. Signing up with your email is only needed to purchase Pro.

Pro is a recurring monthly subscription that unlocks the full analysis (the money breakdown, the 7-day test, competitive research and the Deep Analyze follow-up) on every wazo. Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and merchant of record, who handle billing, invoicing and tax. See our pricing page for current rates and our refund policy for cancellation and refund terms.

A BUILD, PIVOT or KILL verdict is Wazo's opinion, not a guarantee. We're not liable for business decisions made based on it. Use your own judgment, and verify anything material before acting on it.
