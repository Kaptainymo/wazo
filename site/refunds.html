Refund Policy

Updated: October 2026

Cancelling Pro

You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time. You'll keep full Pro access until the end of the billing period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again after that.

Refunds

If you're charged in error, or you're unhappy with Pro within the first 14 days of a charge, email us and we'll refund that charge. Outside that window, we don't offer partial refunds for unused time in a billing period — cancelling simply stops future renewals.

How to request one

Email support@wazo-il.com with the email address your subscription is under. Since Paddle.com is our merchant of record, refunds are processed through them once we approve the request, typically within a few business days.

The free tier

Wazo's free tier has no charge of any kind, so there's nothing to refund there.
