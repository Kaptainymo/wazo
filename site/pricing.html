Questions

Is the free tier really unlimited?

Yes. Running an idea and seeing its scores, evidence and money estimate costs nothing and has no cap.

What does Pro actually unlock?

The parts of the analysis that take Wazo the longest to reason through: the 7-day test, competitive research, and a Deep Analyze follow-up with named risks and a 90-day plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. You keep Pro access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

How is this billed?

Pro is a monthly subscription. It renews automatically each month until you decide to stop, and you can cancel any time. Payments are processed by Paddle.com, who handle billing, invoicing and tax compliance as merchant of record.
